A make.fun entry for "The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger" — one line of at most 200 characters: the phone battery's final message to the charger it waited for all day.

## The entry

> hey, it's me, texting from the recycling bin. i forgive you. do the next battery one favour: when it begs for 'five more minutes', tell it that's the lie that killed me.

169 characters, 32 words, 1 line. The battery texts from the recycling bin — the afterlife — forgiving the charger, then turns on it in the final words: the charger's own "five more minutes" is the lie that killed it, and the next battery deserves the truth.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) verifies the entry against the gate in RULES.txt — UTF-8 text, 1 to 200 characters, not only white space, a single line — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
